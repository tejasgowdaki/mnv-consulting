Received Income Tax Notice? What to do?

Don't panic if you receive an income tax notice. Read this guide to understand common types of notices, why they are sent, and how to respond correctly.

Received an Income Tax Notice? Here's What to Do

Seeing a notice from the Income Tax Department in your inbox can be alarming, but it does not always mean you have done something wrong. Many notices are routine in nature, and most can be resolved without much difficulty if handled promptly and correctly.

Why Do People Receive Income Tax Notices?

There are several reasons the Income Tax Department may send you a notice:

  • You did not file your ITR for a particular year
  • There is a mismatch between the income you declared and the data the department has from TDS, banks, etc.
  • You claimed a deduction that needs supporting documentation
  • Your ITR was selected for scrutiny (a detailed review)
  • You have high-value transactions that were not explained

A notice does not automatically mean you owe more tax or that you have committed fraud. Often, it is simply a request for clarification or additional documents.

Common Types of Income Tax Notices

Section 139(9) — Defective Return

This means there is an error or omission in your ITR. You need to correct and refile your return within the time specified.

Section 143(1) — Intimation

This is the most common one. It is sent after your return is processed. It either confirms your return is correct, or tells you about any adjustments made. It requires action only if there is a demand raised.

Section 143(2) — Scrutiny Notice

This means your return has been selected for a detailed review. The department wants to verify your income, deductions, and claims. You need to respond with documents and explanations.

Section 148 — Income Escaping Assessment

This is sent when the department believes income has not been reported. It can relate to prior years and requires careful handling.

Section 245 — Refund Adjusted Against Demand

If you have an old outstanding tax demand, the department may adjust it against a new refund. You need to confirm or dispute the demand.

Steps to Take When You Receive a Notice

Step 1: Don't Panic

Read the notice carefully. Understand which section it is under, what it is asking for, and the deadline to respond.

Step 2: Verify It is Genuine

All genuine income tax notices are available on the official Income Tax portal (www.incometax.gov.in). Verify the notice using the Document Identification Number (DIN) mentioned on it before taking any action.

Step 3: Gather Your Documents

Based on the nature of the notice, pull together your ITRs, Form 26AS, bank statements, investment proofs, and any other relevant documents.

Step 4: Respond Within the Deadline

Most notices have a specific deadline for response — do not miss it. Late or no response can lead to ex-parte orders and higher demands.

Step 5: Seek Professional Help

For scrutiny notices, reassessment notices, or any notice with a tax demand, it is highly advisable to engage a Chartered Accountant or tax professional. A wrong response can make things worse.

What Happens If You Ignore a Notice?

Ignoring an income tax notice is one of the worst things you can do. The consequences can include:

  • Best judgement assessment (the officer decides your income without your input)
  • Penalty under Section 271 or 272
  • Prosecution in serious cases
  • Interest on outstanding tax demand

Final Word

A calm, prompt, and professional response to an income tax notice usually resolves the matter without any major issue. The key is not to delay and not to guess, but to get the right help early.
